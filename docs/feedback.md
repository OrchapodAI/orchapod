## Nebius AI Cloud

- **Job logs are not available over REST.** Serverless Job logs come from the CLI (`nebius ai job logs <id> --follow`) or the console. A node running on a Job therefore cannot be followed through the API, so our nodes post their logs to the control plane, which must be reachable at a public https URL (ngrok in development). Source: docs.nebius.com/serverless/quickstart/jobs and the `nebius ai job create` reference, as recorded in `hackathon/PLAN.md` (Serverless AI Jobs row); `hackathon/ORCHESTRATOR.md` (section 7); `docs/contracts.md` (NebiusServerless).
- **A Job's timeout defaults to 24 hours.** It can be set from 1 h to 168 h, so we set 3600 s ourselves; left unset, a stuck Job would run for a day. Source: `hackathon/PLAN.md` (Serverless AI Jobs row); `docs/contracts.md` (NebiusServerless, `jobs.create`).
- **No AI Cloud credit is published for the hackathon.** The listed credits are $25 and $25 for Token Factory and $25 for Tavily; GPU time is out of pocket and needs a project with billing and a GPU quota. TODO(D8): when the quota request was filed, the answer and the lead time. Source: `hackathon/PLAN.md` (Credits available now), citing nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com/resources and dev.nebius.com/builders.
- **List prices decide how much GPU a run may use.** gpu-h100-sxm is $4.50 an hour and gpu-l40s-a $1.82 (nebius.com/prices, 2026-10-20), so a full one-hour H100 Job alone exceeds our $3 per-run cap; Lane B is held to about 25 minutes of H100 plus at most 30 minutes of L40S. Whether Serverless AI bills at these rates is unverified: TODO(D8) the rate from the first real Job. ## NVIDIA Nemotron models

The numbers below come from one spike on a rented vast.ai A100 80 GB, outside Nebius (the first 200 rows of yahma/alpaca-cleaned, 60 steps). They are inputs to a decision, not claims about Nebius.

- **Llama-3.1-Nemotron-Nano-8B-v1 was an easy LoRA student.** With Unsloth 16-bit LoRA, 60 steps took 62.5 s at a 15.9 GiB peak, the merge to 16-bit took 40.5 s (15 GB) and vLLM 0.18.0 was healthy 51 s after start. It needed no extra kernels or dependency pins. Source: `containers/unsloth-train/spike/RESULTS.md` (Main run); `hackathon/briefs/2026-10-20-ml-engineering-spike4-report.md` (DECISION).
- **The 30B-A3B hybrid (Mamba and MoE) was much harder.** PEFT 0.21 refuses LoRA on Mamba `out_proj` and `conv1d` for `nemotron_h`. The router is a raw `nn.Parameter` that LoRA cannot wrap, so it and the 128 routed experts stayed frozen. Installing `mamba_ssm` pulls `tilelang`, which downgrades `apache-tvm-ffi` and breaks vLLM's xgrammar import. The Hugging Face remote code loops over the experts, so training ran about 11 s a step against 1 s for the 8B, with the GPU about 38% busy, and peaked at 60.5 GiB. Source: `containers/unsloth-train/spike/RESULTS.md` (Stretch, Surprises 2 to 4).
- **Three different switches turn reasoning off.** Nemotron Nano 8B v1 took the system prompt "detailed thinking off", the 30B-A3B took `enable_thinking=false` in the chat template, and Token Factory's OpenAPI lists `reasoning_effort`. Source: `containers/unsloth-train/spike/RESULTS.md` (both completions); `hackathon/briefs/2026-10-20-contracts-platform.md` (answer 3).
